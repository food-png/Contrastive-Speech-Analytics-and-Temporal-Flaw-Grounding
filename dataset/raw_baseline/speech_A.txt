Today I want to tell you three stories from my life. The first story is about connecting the dots. You cannot connect the dots looking forward. You can only connect them looking backward. So you have to trust that the dots will somehow connect in your future. You have to trust in something: your gut, destiny, life, karma, whatever. Because believing that the dots will connect down the road will give you the confidence to follow your heart and intuition. Somehow they already know what you truly want to become. Everything else is secondary. Stay hungry. Stay foolish.
