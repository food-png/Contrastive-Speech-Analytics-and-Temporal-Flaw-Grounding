The night was wide and quiet, and the river carried old secrets downstream. She stood at the edge and listened to the wind braid through the reeds. Memory rose like mist: a childhood kitchen, warm bread, a lullaby half-forgotten. Grief and gratitude arrived together, neither asking permission. She spoke softly to the dark, naming every fear she had outgrown. And when the first light touched the water, her voice found its full range again — low as thunder, bright as glass, unbroken.
